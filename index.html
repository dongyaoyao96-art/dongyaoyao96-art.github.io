读懂儿童如何一起创造Understanding How Children Create Together

有人说，生命的尊贵，在人海里。而我想说，数学创造的尊贵，在每个孩子的身上。如果仔细去看每一张面孔，就会注意到每一个有尊严的孩子，用看似稚嫩的话语，生机勃勃地创造着属于他们的数学意义。

Some say the dignity of life is found among the many. I would say that the dignity of mathematical creativity resides in every child. If we look closely at each face, we will notice every child, with their own dignity, using seemingly simple words to create mathematical meanings of their own with vibrant energy.

——董瑶瑶— Yaoyao Dong

01 / About

关于我About Me

研究方向Research Focus

我的研究以团队创造力为核心，重点关注小学数学中的创新意识与团队创造力，以及STEM和跨学科学习中的团队创造力，围绕这些领域开展理论、测评与培养研究。

My research centers on group creativity, including creative awareness and group creativity in primary mathematics, and group creativity in STEM and interdisciplinary learning. My work encompasses theory, assessment and cultivation.

教材与课堂Curriculum & Classroom Research

我长期深入小学数学教材编写与课堂教学一线，参与北师大版小学数学教材（第五版）及教师用书建设，开展教材循证建设、“一节好课”和教师实践性知识生成研究，并参与骨干教师培养与区域教研合作。

I work closely with primary mathematics textbook development and classroom practice, contributing to the fifth-edition BNU primary mathematics textbooks and teacher resources. I also study evidence-informed textbook development, high-quality lessons and teachers’ practical knowledge, alongside teacher development and regional collaboration.

教育评价Educational Assessment

我还参与区域大规模教育测评与教育质量监测，关注学生数学学习表现、创造力发展的学校支持环境及教育质量改进，推动教育研究与课堂实践相互促进。

I also engage in large-scale regional assessment and educational quality monitoring, focusing on students’ mathematical learning, school environments that support creativity, and evidence-informed educational improvement.

RESEARCH 01Primary Mathematics

小学数学创新意识与团队创造力Creative Awareness & Group Creativity in Primary Mathematics

Theory · Assessment · Cultivation

围绕小学数学创新意识与团队创造力，研究其理论内涵、表现评价与培养路径。Exploring the theory, assessment and cultivation of creative awareness and group creativity in primary mathematics.

理论建构Theory测评与评价Assessment教学培养Cultivation
展开研究重点Research priorities
  • 构建小学数学创新意识与团队数学创造力的概念框架和评价维度。
  • 开发适合儿童的开放性数学任务、团队创造力评价量规与测评工具。
  • 研究小学数学课堂中创新意识与团队创造力的培养策略和教学实践。
  • Developing conceptual and assessment frameworks for creative awareness and group mathematical creativity.
  • Designing open-ended tasks, assessment rubrics and age-appropriate group creativity measures.
  • Investigating teaching approaches that foster creative awareness and group creativity.
RESEARCH 02STEM & Interdisciplinary

STEM与跨学科学习中的团队创造力Group Creativity in STEM & Interdisciplinary Learning

Theory · Assessment · Cultivation

关注团队如何整合数学、科学、技术与工程等学科实践，探索跨学科学习中团队创造力的测评与培养。Studying how groups integrate mathematics, science, technology and engineering, with a focus on group creativity assessment and cultivation in interdisciplinary learning.

STEM教育STEM Education跨学科实践Interdisciplinary Practice团队创造力Group Creativity
展开研究重点Research priorities
  • 探索STEM及跨学科学习中团队创造力的理论与评价框架。
  • 开发多学科协同的团队创造力任务、评价指标与测评工具。
  • 开展基于真实问题的学习活动和教学实践，促进团队创造力发展。
  • Investigating conceptual and assessment frameworks for group creativity in STEM and interdisciplinary learning.
  • Designing interdisciplinary group creativity tasks, performance indicators and assessment tools.
  • Exploring authentic problem-solving tasks and teaching approaches that support creative groups.
RESEARCH 03Curriculum & Teaching

小学数学教材与课堂教学研究Primary Mathematics Textbooks & Classroom Teaching

Evidence-Informed Textbooks · Quality Teaching · Teacher Learning

研究小学数学教材的循证建设与课堂实施，围绕“一节好课”评价、教师实践性知识生成及教师专业发展开展持续研究。Researching evidence-informed mathematics textbook development and classroom implementation, with a focus on evaluating high-quality lessons, teachers’ practical knowledge, and professional learning.

教材循证建设Evidence-Informed Textbooks“一节好课”研究Quality Lesson Research教师实践性知识Teacher Practical Knowledge
展开研究重点Research priorities
  • 教材研究：围绕小学数学教材与教师用书的需求、编写架构、试学试教及实施效果开展研究，并参与北师大版小学数学教材（第五版）与教师用书的编写和实验。
  • “一节好课”研究：基于课堂录像构建好课评价模型与可观察、可判定的课堂观察工具，关注教材使用、学生主体性和必要学习过程。
  • 教师实践性知识生成研究：追踪优秀青年教师的课堂实践发展，考察持续研修中教学认识、课堂决策与实践性知识的变化。
  • Textbook research: Investigating teacher-guide needs, textbook design, classroom piloting and implementation, alongside contributions to the fifth-edition BNU primary mathematics textbooks and teacher resources.
  • High-quality lesson research: Developing an observation-based evaluation framework grounded in classroom video, with attention to textbook use, student agency and essential learning processes.
  • Teacher practical knowledge: Examining how teachers’ professional knowledge and classroom decisions develop through sustained lesson-study and professional learning.
RESEARCH 04Assessment & Evaluation

教育测评与教育质量监测研究Educational Assessment & Quality Monitoring

Learning Assessment · Creative Environments · Large-Scale Monitoring

结合数学学习表现、形成性评价与创造力支持环境的实证研究，开展区域大规模测评、学校环境评价与教育质量监测。Combining empirical studies of mathematical learning, formative assessment, and creativity-supportive school environments with regional large-scale assessment and educational quality monitoring.

数学学习评价Mathematics Assessment创造力学校环境Creative School Environments大规模质量监测Large-Scale Monitoring
展开研究重点Research priorities
  • 数学学习与评价：通过大规模数据研究小学生统计推理、数学学业表现及教师评价信念，关注形成性评价与学生创造力的关系。
  • 学校创造力环境测评：研制支持学生创造力发展的学校环境评价指标，关注不同学段与学校群体的表现及其变化。
  • 区域教育质量监测：参与小学数学教育质量监测与区域教育评价研究，以实证证据支持教育改进和评价改革。
  • Mathematical learning and assessment: Using large-scale data to examine statistical reasoning, mathematics achievement and teachers’ assessment-related beliefs, including links between formative assessment and creativity.
  • Creativity-supportive environments: Developing indicators of school environments that foster creativity and examining differences and changes across grades and school contexts.
  • Regional quality monitoring: Conducting educational assessment and primary mathematics quality monitoring to inform evidence-based educational improvement and evaluation reform.
