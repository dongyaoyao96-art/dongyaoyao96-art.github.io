读懂儿童如何一起创造Understanding How Children Create Together

有人说，生命的尊贵，在人海里。而我想说，数学创造的尊贵，在每个孩子的身上。如果仔细去看每一张面孔，就会注意到每一个有尊严的孩子，用看似稚嫩的话语，生机勃勃地创造着属于他们的数学意义。

Some say the dignity of life is found among the many. I would say that the dignity of mathematical creativity resides in every child. If we look closely at each face, we will notice every child, with their own dignity, using seemingly simple words to create mathematical meanings of their own with vibrant energy.

——董瑶瑶— Yaoyao Dong

01 / About

关于我About Me

我是北京师范大学教育学博士研究生，国内博士生导师为刘坚教授。2024年11月至2025年12月，获国家留学基金委资助，赴爱尔兰都柏林城市大学心理学系联合培养，外方导师为Vlad Glăveanu教授。

I am a PhD candidate in Education at Beijing Normal University, supervised by Professor Jian Liu. From November 2024 to December 2025, I undertook CSC-funded joint doctoral training at the School of Psychology, Dublin City University, hosted by Professor Vlad Glăveanu.

我的研究以团队创造力为核心，主要关注小学数学领域的创新意识与团队创造力，以及STEM和跨学科学习中的团队创造力，围绕这些领域开展理论、测评与培养研究。

My research centers on group creativity, with two connected areas of interest: creative awareness and group creativity in primary mathematics, and group creativity in STEM and interdisciplinary learning. Across these areas, I focus on theory, assessment and educational practices for fostering creativity.

我深入小学数学课程教材与教学一线，作为北师大版小学数学教材（第五版）编写成员与五年级教师用书分册副主编，深度参与新版教材及教师用书编写与实验的全过程。依托教材编写组平台，我担任“全国新世纪小学数学杰出人才发展工程高研班”指导专家，带领骨干教师开展小学数学教育教学研究，并与北京、深圳、内蒙古、辽宁、河南、浙江等地的资深小学数学教研员开展深度科研合作与实践交流。

I work closely with schools and teachers on mathematics curriculum development. As a contributing author of the fifth edition of the BNU primary mathematics textbooks and deputy editor of the Grade 5 teacher’s guide, I have been extensively involved throughout the development and classroom piloting of the revised textbooks and teacher resources. Through the textbook development group, I serve as an academic mentor for the National New Century Primary Mathematics Outstanding Talent Development Programme, supporting experienced teachers' professional learning and collaborating with senior mathematics education specialists in Beijing, Shenzhen, Inner Mongolia, Liaoning, Henan and Zhejiang.

此外，我在区域层面参与大规模教育测评与教育质量监测，重点关注学生学习表现、创造力发展的学校支持环境及教育质量改进，推动研究与教育实践相互促进。

I also work on large-scale educational assessment and quality monitoring at the regional level, with interests in student learning, school environments that support creativity, and evidence-informed educational improvement.

RESEARCH 01Group Creativity

儿童团队数学创造过程Children's Group Mathematical Creativity

Group Mathematical Creative Process (GMCP)

解释儿童新想法如何在团队中的数学化行动、深度互动和产物迭代中逐步获得新的数学意义。Examining how children's ideas acquire new mathematical meanings through mathematizing actions, deep interaction, and iterative artifact development in groups.

5As数学化Mathematizing路径与模式Pathways & Patterns
查看研究内容与发现Research details and findings
  • 从5As社会文化创造力视角界定儿童团队数学创造。
  • 识别五条新想法发展路径：独立、协同、分工数学化及其与深度互动的交融。
  • 围绕产物迭代发现“单—单”“双—单”“单—双”“双—双”四类过程模式。
  • Conceptualizing group mathematical creativity through the sociocultural 5As lens.
  • Identifying five pathways of idea development involving individual, collaborative, and distributed mathematizing, with or without deep interaction.
  • Tracing four process patterns across artifact iteration: single–single, dual–single, single–dual, and dual–dual.
